How it works

Follow the money

1
The guest pays the aggregatorBy card, wallet or app
2
The bank takes its fee firstEvery single transaction has a processing cost
3
The aggregator takes its commissionThis pays for everything that brought you the guest
4
You get your payoutOn a set cycle: weekly, bi-weekly or monthly

Why 0% is impossible

At 0%, they pay to serve you

Guest pays
EGP 500
Bank fee (e.g. 2.5%)
Taken before anyone else is paid
− EGP 12.5
At 0% commission, the aggregator loses money on every order you get. No business signs that deal.

How to negotiate

Give something, get something

1
Bring data, not feelings"Our average order is X, we can do Y orders a month"
2
Offer something in returnExclusivity in your area, a volume commitment, promos
3
Get it in writingRates, tiers, payout dates, who pays refunds
4
Review every 3–6 monthsYour numbers change, so should your deal
