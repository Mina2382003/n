The clock · 2/3

Work out your time zone yourself

Longitude ÷ 15 = hours from UTC

The Earth turns 360° in 24 hours, so every 15° is one hour. East is ahead (+), west is behind (−).

UTC+2Cairo: 31°E ÷ 15 ≈ 2
UTC−5New York: 74°W ÷ 15 ≈ 5
But borders are political: China spans about 5 zones and uses just one (UTC+8). India uses UTC+5:30.

The 2 norths · 1/3

Your compass is lying (a little)

★ True North
The geographic North Pole, where Earth's axis meets the surface. It doesn't move. Maps are drawn to it.
🧭 Magnetic North
Where your compass needle points. It moves, because the liquid iron in Earth's outer core keeps shifting.
Magnetic north has drifted from northern Canada towards Siberia, at up to about 50 km a year.
