The one-page concept

4 questions before anything else

👥 WhoWho exactly is your guest? Age, budget, occasion
🍽️ WhatCuisine, signature dishes, price range
📍 WhereWhich area, which street, who's already there
💡 WhyWhy would they pick you over the place next door?
If you can't answer these on one page, you're not ready to spend money yet.

Step 2 · Your sentence

Your concept in one sentence

We serve [cuisine] for [guest] in [area] at [price], and they choose us because [difference].
Example
"We serve modern Egyptian grills for young families in New Cairo at mid-range prices, and they choose us because we have a kids' corner and a 20-minute lunch."
