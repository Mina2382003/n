# Reel editing rules (from the channel owner)

Every reel in `reels/` must have all of these. A reel missing any of them is rejected.

1. **Analyze the video first.** Transcribe it (sherpa-onnx Whisper from GitHub releases works here; HuggingFace is blocked)
   and decide what it is about before choosing anything.
2. **Verbal hook**: open on his own strongest line (a cold open lifted from the clip), with an English card of that line.
3. **Visual hook** in the first second: something that stops the scroll and matches the topic (snap zoom + bold English
   title, a product/phone mockup, a breaking-news banner, a ticket, ...).
4. **CTA in English**, always. Never an Arabic CTA.
5. **Only the effects that fit the content.** Pick 2-4 from the list (Orbit, Chat Takeover, Frame Escape, Snap Zoom,
   Checklist Pop, Headline Storm, Zoom Portal, Growth Curve, Studio Reveal, B-roll Overlay, Paper Cutout, Dolly Zoom,
   Magazine Cover, Liquid Glass, Zoom Through Cut, Green Screen, Frame Break, Digital Zoom, Phone Mockup, Checklist),
   each tied to what he is saying at that moment. No effects for their own sake.
6. On-screen text and callouts in English; keep overlays off his face.
